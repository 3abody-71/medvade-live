# Residency readiness research

The product uses a **Residency Readiness Index**, not an individual match-probability model. The reviewed official sources do not provide a universal formula that converts an IMG's examination results, research, electives, references, visa status, specialty, and graduation timing into a guaranteed or statistically valid personal match probability. The UI therefore uses a transparent preparation measure: verified checks count fully, in-progress checks count halfway, and unknown/not-met checks do not count. Hard eligibility gates are kept separate from competitive/context evidence.

## United States

The questionnaire covers Step 1 and Step 2 CK, ECFMG Pathways/OET, credential verification, U.S. clinical experience/electives, research/publications, ERAS documents and letters, visa feasibility, and programme-specific requirements. Step 1/Step 2 CK passing status and ECFMG certification are eligibility gates; research and electives are recorded as programme-context evidence because there is no universal national threshold.

Sources: [ECFMG certification pathways](https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/), [NRMP IMG eligibility](https://www.nrmp.org/residency-applicants/get-ready-for-the-match/are-you-eligible/), [AAMC ERAS documents](https://students-residents.aamc.org/applying-residencies-eras/documents-eras-residency-applicants), and [ECFMG J-1 requirements](https://www.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html).

## United Kingdom

The questionnaire covers GMC qualification/registration, PLAB or another applicable route, English evidence, clinical competence, research/teaching/audit, right to work, and the named specialty person specification. NHS specialty training criteria vary by specialty, level, year, and recruitment process. Broad competition ratios must not be presented as individual match probabilities.

Sources: [GMC English evidence](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply-guide-for-doctors/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english), [NHS specialty-training eligibility](https://medical.hee.nhs.uk/medical-training-recruitment/medical-specialty-training/overview-of-specialty-training/eligibility), [NHS IMT 2026 person specification](https://medical.hee.nhs.uk/medical-training-recruitment/medical-specialty-training/person-specifications/person-specifications-2026/internal-medicine-training-ct1-2026), and [UK Health and Care Worker visa](https://www.gov.uk/health-care-worker-visa).

## Australia

The questionnaire covers eligible medical qualification and IMG pathway, AMC assessments, Ahpra English evidence, verification/registration/supervision, Australian clinical exposure, research/references, and visa. Recruitment is decentralized across states, hospitals, colleges, and programmes; AMC assessment or registration does not guarantee a training place.

Sources: [Medical Board IMG pathways](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Registration/International-Medical-Graduates.aspx), [AMC standard-pathway assessments](https://www.amc.org.au/pathways/standard-pathway/amc-assessments/), [Ahpra English standard](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills.aspx), and [Australian visa finder](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-finder).

## Canada

The questionnaire covers CaRMS citizenship/PR and R-1 eligibility, MCCQE Part I/NAC, language, source verification, provincial assessments, Canadian clinical exposure/electives, research/references, programme fit, and work authorization. Province and programme requirements differ materially, and CaRMS aggregate reports are descriptive rather than individual predictions.

Sources: [CaRMS R-1 eligibility](https://www.carms.ca/match/r-1-main-residency-match/eligibility-criteria/), [CaRMS MCC exam requirements](https://www.carms.ca/match/r-1-main-residency-match/eligibility-criteria/r-1-summary-of-mcc-examination-requirements/), [MCC credential verification](https://mcc.ca/credentials-and-services/services/verification-of-credentials/), and [CaRMS data reports](https://www.carms.ca/data-reports/r1-data-reports/).

## Germany

The questionnaire covers qualification recognition, Kenntnisprüfung, B2 general German, C1 medical German, Approbation/Berufserlaubnis, Land and chamber rules, clinical experience, research/references, specialty training, and residence/work authorization. German specialty training is generally employment- and state-chamber-based rather than a national match; the model training regulation is not legally binding.

Sources: [Make it in Germany physician guidance](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-search-application/professions-in-demand/physicians), [German Medical Association recognition](https://www.bundesaerztekammer.de/en/work-and-training-in-germany/recognition), [model training regulation](https://www.bundesaerztekammer.de/themen/aerzte/aus-fort-und-weiterbildung/aerztliche-weiterbildung/muster-weiterbildungsordnung), and [EU Blue Card](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/working-and-living-in-germany/iss-en/issue-03-2026/blue-card).

## Ireland

The questionnaire covers Medical Council route, internship and route-specific exams, English evidence, EPIC/good standing, clinical experience/electives, research/references, Medical Council registration, named programme requirements, and work authorization. Ireland has multiple postgraduate training bodies and no reviewed national IMG match-probability model.

Sources: [Medical Council English evidence](https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html), [Medical Council first-time registration](https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/), [EPIC Ireland instructions](https://www.ecfmg.org/psv/instructions-ireland.html), and [Irish doctors employment permits](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/doctors/).

For other MedVade countries, the feature provides a general interview covering qualification, language, licensing, verification, clinical experience, research, programme fit, and work authorization using the country record's licensing authority link. Those fallback questions are intentionally not presented as country-specific legal advice until the relevant official pathway is researched and added.
